# PlaneByte Cancellation Policy

Owner approved all four revised policies on 17 November 2026 as planebyte-subscriptions-2026-11-17-v2. Publication is recorded in the release evidence.

## Cancel automatic renewal

For a provider-confirmed recurring subscription, open Settings → Billing & Subscription and choose Cancel automatic renewal, or use the secure provider billing portal. No support contact is required. Review the confirmed effective date. Cancellation must be confirmed by the provider; a pending or failed request is not shown as successful. Billing and cancellation remain accessible even if product access is restricted.

For a72-hour card trial, cancel before the displayed first-charge date/time to prevent the selected monthly paid plan from starting. Once cancellation is provider-confirmed, no later automatic charge is authorized by that subscription. Trial or current paid access continues to its agreed end unless mandatory rights, a verified refund or a security restriction require otherwise. A confirmation is shown and sent by transactional email.

Legacy one-time PayU purchases do not automatically renew and never authorize a recurring card mandate. Their saved start/end dates and original manual-renewal terms remain unchanged.

## Canceled or failed checkout

If hosted checkout is canceled, fails or remains unverified, your onboarding stays saved at Choose Your Plan & Payment. No trial or paid access is granted. You may reconcile or safely retry the saved checkout. Reaching a success URL does not activate a subscription. Canceling checkout does not refund an independently captured payment; use the refund-review workflow where applicable.

## Provider, plan changes and payment failures

Subscription billing country and provider are verified server-side. You cannot silently switch an existing subscription between Creem and PayU by editing a country field. Plan changes must disclose the provider amount, effective date, recurring terms and any approved proration before authorization. We do not invent INR prices or activate unapproved PayU mandates.

For failed renewal, use Billing & Subscription or the secure provider portal to review payment recovery. Product operations may be restricted while the provider reports unpaid, past-due or expired access; authentication, billing and cancellation remain available. Yearly checkout is unavailable until approved.

## Account closure and data

You may request account closure, a full export or erasure review through Settings → Account → Sign-in & Security or contact@planebyte.com. The request is recorded and reviewed for identity, workspace authority and retained-record obligations. It does not immediately delete data, remove another user's records or issue a refund. Ordinary account data is scheduled for deletion/anonymization within 30 days after verified closure, with backup aging targeted within 90 days, subject to justified exceptions in the Privacy Policy.

Expiry of access alone preserves saved research under the applicable retention policy. Closure of a personal account and the handling of an organization's workspace are separate matters; organizational authority must be verified. Contact support before closure if you need help exporting records or transferring authorized workspace ownership.

For refund review, use the separate Refund Policy, including its seven-day initial-purchase request window and any additional mandatory rights. For questions or escalation, contact contact@planebyte.com with the order or request reference. No password, OTP or card details are required in that message.
