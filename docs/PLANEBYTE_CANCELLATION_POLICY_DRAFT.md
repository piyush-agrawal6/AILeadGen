# PlaneByte Cancellation Policy

## One-time prepaid access

The current service uses one-time purchases for fixed-term access. A monthly purchase provides exactly 30 days. The yearly model is one calendar year, but yearly checkout is unavailable until exact prices and allowance timing are approved. Neither option creates an automatically renewing subscription or a recurring auto-debit mandate.

You may choose not to renew at any time. No cancellation action is needed to stop an automatic charge because the current offering does not make automatic renewal charges. Access continues until the end of the term already purchased, unless a verified refund, security restriction or other lawful agreement condition applies. A renewal reminder does not authorize a payment or extend the term.

## Hosted checkout cancellation

Leaving or cancelling an in-progress PayU checkout does not establish whether a bank captured funds. Review the payment history and allow server-side reconciliation. A cancellation screen does not refund an already captured payment, and an uncertain result should be checked before creating another purchase.

## Renewals and plan changes

An early manual renewal starts after existing paid access ends. A separately purchased different plan also begins after existing paid access ends. There is no current early-upgrade, proration or unused-time credit policy. Choosing not to make another renewal does not cancel a separately purchased future term; request review under the Refund Policy if needed.

## Account closure and data

You may request account closure, a full export or erasure review through Settings → Account → Sign-in & Security or contact@planebyte.com. The request is recorded and reviewed for identity, workspace authority and retained-record obligations. It does not immediately delete data, remove another user's records or issue a refund. Ordinary account data is scheduled for deletion/anonymization within 30 days after verified closure, with backup aging targeted within 90 days, subject to justified exceptions in the Privacy Policy.

Expiry of access alone preserves saved research under the applicable retention policy. Closure of a personal account and the handling of an organization's workspace are separate matters; organizational authority must be verified. Contact support before closure if you need help exporting records or transferring authorized workspace ownership.

For refund review, use the separate Refund Policy, including its seven-day initial-purchase request window and any additional mandatory rights. For questions or escalation, contact contact@planebyte.com with the order or request reference. No password, OTP or card details are required in that message.
