# Public service disclosures — owner review draft

This is an unpublished operational draft. It does not replace the public development pages or establish an approved legal agreement. Contracting identity, address, refunds, retention, governing law and the rights process require owner/legal approval before enabling signup or paid checkout.

## Service and account access

PlaneByte currently offers Intent Radar and Growth Intelligence. New workspace owners register using a company-managed email address and verify mailbox control. Google sign-in uses Google's verified identity; an email/password account uses a securely hashed password and an email verification code. Verification of an individual mailbox does not establish legal ownership of a company domain. Joining another workspace requires an explicit invitation and current membership checks.

Account sessions expire after eight hours and may be revoked. Password reset revokes existing account sessions. Google-only users recover access through Google unless they explicitly configure a PlaneByte password. Keep account credentials and developer keys private. Lead Engine is preserved for a future release and is not included in this offering.

## Trial and purchases

A qualifying verified company account receives one seven-day trial: five discovery searches, three shared AI operations, 150,000 reserved AI tokens, one manual monitor and one team member. Scheduled checks, reports, developer API and MCP access require paid access. No card or automatic renewal applies to the trial. One trial is available per verified account and company domain; an invitation does not create another trial.

Approved monthly list prices are Starter USD 59, Growth USD 149 and Scale USD 379. Each one-time monthly purchase provides exactly 30 days of access. Enterprise is by separate agreement. Yearly prices and allowance timing are not yet approved, so yearly checkout is unavailable. USD checkout remains closed until the specific PayU account is verified to support USD charging. No GST is collected while PlaneByte is not GST-registered; no GSTIN is required from the purchaser.

PayU hosts payment entry. PlaneByte creates and verifies each order on the server and grants paid access only after independent confirmation of a matching captured payment. A pending, failed, cancelled, browser-reported or unverified payment does not grant paid allowances. Payment history preserves provider, currency and test/live classification. A payment receipt is not a GST tax invoice.

A paid purchase immediately replaces any remaining trial. Manual renewal before expiry adds the next term after existing paid access ends. A different plan also starts after existing paid access ends. No automatic recurring mandate, early upgrade, proration or unused-time credit is offered. Cancelling a hosted payment is separate from requesting a refund of a completed purchase. Access expires at the recorded time; saved research and accounting history remain retained under the approved retention policy.

**Refund policy decision required:** specify the actual eligibility window, service-delivery exceptions, handling of duplicate charges and billing errors, and the process/timing for an owner-reviewed refund. The implementation records refund requests without promising approval or moving money. Full refunds independently confirmed in the original currency revoke the refunded purchase's access. Partial refunds or missing currency evidence require review. No universal no-refund statement or guaranteed bank settlement time is assumed.

## Research and AI disclosures

Research is limited to operational sources with the required authorization. Coverage and results vary; a source record is evidence, not a guarantee of accuracy, employment, buying intent or commercial outcome. AI suggestions can be wrong. Users review company descriptions, targeting profiles and proposed actions before saving or executing them. Setup does not automatically launch paid research, outreach or payment activity. Public availability of source material does not override publisher restrictions.

## Privacy and processing facts

PlaneByte processes account and workspace identity, company profiles, reviewed targeting information, saved research, usage and security records. Requested AI operations send the necessary task context to the configured AI provider. The current deployment uses Vercel for the application frontend, Google Cloud for API/workers and Vertex AI, Neon PostgreSQL for stored data, and PayU for hosted payments. Resend is the intended transactional delivery provider; real backend delivery and webhook configuration are not yet accepted. The marketing website uses Netlify separately. These are deployment facts, not a complete approved subprocessor or international-transfer disclosure.

Verification and recovery messages use short-lived credentials. The application does not log OTPs, store plaintext passwords or collect card details. Workspace access is checked against active server-side membership. Security, contact suppression, accounting and migration history must not be deleted as a beta cleanup shortcut.

**Privacy decisions required:** identify the controller and service address; approve retention periods, account closure/export/deletion handling, privacy-request contact and response process, and international processing disclosures. Do not promise deletion times, certifications, uptime, a governing law or contractual liability limits that have not been approved.
