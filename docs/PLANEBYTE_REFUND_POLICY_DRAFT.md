# PlaneByte Refund Policy

**Unpublished publication draft — contracting identity verification required.** Prepared November 16, 2026. Operator: **[verified legal contracting name and official address required]**. This draft reflects the owner's proposed launch policy and must be checked against applicable obligations before publication.

## Initial purchases

You may request a refund for an initial prepaid purchase within seven days of verified payment. Submit Request refund review in Settings → Billing & Usage, or email contact@planebyte.com with your order reference and the reason. Do not send passwords, OTPs or card details. The policy applies to the initial purchase; additional rights required by applicable law remain available for every purchase.

The request is reviewed against the actual purchase, payment evidence, service circumstances and applicable rights. The seven-day window is not an unreviewed promise that every request has already been approved. No undisclosed usage-percentage condition is imposed. If a request cannot be approved in full, we explain the reason and the available support/escalation route. No blanket exclusion of mandatory rights is intended.

## Billing errors, duplicates and uncertain payments

Contact us about an incorrect or duplicate charge even if you are unsure whether the initial-purchase window applies. We reconcile the specific transactions before deciding what action is appropriate. A pending payment is not assumed successful. Check your payment history and contact support before paying again when a prior result is uncertain.

A recorded request is an acknowledgement of review, not proof that money was refunded. PayU processes the financial transaction. A refund is treated as completed only after independent provider verification; actual banking credit timing can vary. We do not promise a settlement deadline that the provider has not confirmed.

## Renewal, future terms and yearly access

Access is prepaid for a fixed term and does not automatically renew. A separately purchased renewal or future term is a separate transaction, subject to its agreement and applicable rights. No automatic unused-time credit, early-upgrade proration or automatic refund of an unused future term is promised. You may still request review, and mandatory rights remain preserved.

Yearly checkout is unavailable until exact yearly prices and allowance timing are approved and published. This policy does not invent a yearly amount or discount. Once yearly purchases are enabled, the published initial-purchase request window and any additional applicable rights must be presented with those terms before purchase.

## Access and retained records

A full refund independently confirmed in the original purchase currency revokes the access attributable to that purchase. It does not delete saved research, another purchase or the original accounting record. Partial refunds and missing currency evidence require review; they do not automatically create a new entitlement or a credit balance. Payment/refund and other justified retention exceptions are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Contact contact@planebyte.com with the case/order reference for status or escalation. Nothing in this policy limits rights that applicable law does not permit us to exclude.
