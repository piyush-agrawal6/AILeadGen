## 3. Why information is used

We use the information needed to provide the service you request: verify and secure accounts; provision the correct workspace and roles; preserve onboarding progress; run authorized research and AI tasks; save and retrieve results; account for usage; verify payments; activate and expire fixed-term access; reconcile refunds; deliver necessary account/billing messages; handle support; and maintain the service.

We also use appropriate records to prevent fraud and abuse, investigate incidents and disputes, enforce source and workspace permissions, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required, we obtain it for the specified purpose and provide the applicable withdrawal route. Where another lawful basis is permitted and appropriate, processing is limited to that basis. Acceptance of general Terms is not treated as blanket consent to unrelated marketing or every future use.

Research relevance or intent labels are interpretations of available evidence. They are not conclusive decisions about an individual or a guarantee that someone wants to be contacted. Workspace users must review results and ensure their subsequent use is lawful.

## 7. Cookies and local preferences

The application uses essential session cookies and security mechanisms for authentication and protection of account actions. It may store functional preferences such as theme, navigation state and workspace context. Production authentication cookies are protected against ordinary client-side access and use secure transport settings.

Disabling essential cookies can prevent sign-in and account functions from working. A theme preference is separate from permission to perform research, access a source, process a payment or send outreach. Any nonessential analytics or advertising technology requires its own accurate disclosure and applicable consent controls; no advertising or analytics consent is bundled into an account verification code.

## 8. Transactional messages and communication choices

Account verification, password recovery, explicit team invitations, payment confirmations, renewal/expiry notices and important security messages support the requested service. Delivery is bounded, deduplicated and subject to suppression and authorization checks. A queued message does not establish that it arrived; delivery and complaint events may be processed from the configured email provider.

General workspace notifications require the relevant enabled feature and recipient preferences. Bulk outreach and marketing are separate from transactional messages and are not authorized merely by enabling account email delivery. An opt-out or suppression record may need to be retained so the choice continues to be honored. The approved deletion process below will explain the scope of such retention.

## 9. Retention after expiry or account closure

Fixed-term access expires at its recorded time without automatically deleting saved research, payment history or other retained records. Workspace closure, personal account closure, expiry, membership removal and a verified deletion request have different effects.

After verified account closure, ordinary account data is scheduled for deletion or anonymization within 30 days, subject to justified exceptions below. Backup removal follows the normal retention cycle, targeted at no more than 90 days after verified closure. These targets come from the owner’s launch policy. The operator records closure and its deadlines; submitting a request alone does not execute a purge. Existing workspace exports and a personal account-detail download are available, and a fuller export can be requested for review.

Retention must account for the purpose and applicable obligations for each record category. Accounting/refund records, consent and security records, legal holds/disputes, contact/message history and suppression information can require different treatment from an unused research draft. Backups are recovery copies; any eventual deletion process must define how deletions remain effective after recovery and how retained backup copies age out, without promising immediate removal from every copy.

## 10. Requests, correction, withdrawal and deletion

Depending on applicable law and your relationship with the workspace, you may have rights to obtain information about processing, access or correct your personal information, request erasure, withdraw consent, raise a grievance, or use other rights available to you. A workspace administrator can help with organizational records within that administrator's authority. Applicable rights are not limited to the application controls currently available.

Use Settings → Account → Sign-in & Security to submit an authenticated full-export, account-closure/deletion or erasure-review request, or contact contact@planebyte.com. The application acknowledges the request with a reference. Support reviews identity, workspace authority and retention exceptions, requests proportionate clarification where needed, and records verified closure and the applicable 30/90-day targets. Use the case reference with the same contact for status, correction or escalation. Request handling also observes any additional applicable legal requirements. No named privacy officer or legally required designation is invented.

Use the approved channel and provide enough context to locate the relevant account/workspace without sending a password, OTP or unnecessary identity document. Verification should be proportionate and prevent one person from deleting another person's or organization's records. We will explain any valid reason a request cannot be completed in full and any available escalation or review route under the approved process and applicable law.

Deletion or withdrawal may affect features that need the relevant information. Retention may remain necessary for payment/refund and legal records, fraud/security investigations, dispute resolution, lawful holds, contact history or suppression choices. No blanket exception permits keeping every record indefinitely, and no request automatically authorizes a production reset or deletion of another workspace. Closing access is not a substitute for implementing and verifying an approved deletion request.
